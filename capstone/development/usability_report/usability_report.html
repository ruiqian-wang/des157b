Signigicant Observations

1. One user wasn’t sure if the “Get Started” button on the banner was clickable or just decorative. The flat, almost paper-like style made it feel like part of the illustration instead of a real button. Could use some subtle hover effects or more button-like styling to signal interactivity.

2. One user didn’t really get why they were being asked “Do you want to be part of that future?” It felt too broad and disconnected from the content they just explored. Maybe needs a little lead-in sentence or a hint about what happens next after clicking.

3. One user mentioned that the timeline section just looks like text on a scroll bar, and doesn’t visually feel like a timeline. And suggested adding more visual timeline cues, for example dates or markers

Other Notes

• Users were curious what the “Interactive 3D model” would actually do. It might be helpful to show a preview image or add a hint about what kind of learning world they’re about to explore.

• The transition between sections could be smoother with some scroll transitions.

• Colors and typography are nice and clean overall.
